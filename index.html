TicTacPro: Exactly Solving a Combinatorial Piece-Placement Game and Stress-Testing Deep Q-Learning Against It

Exact game solving meets deep reinforcement learning.

An exhaustive alpha-beta solver proves TicTacPro is a first-player win from every one of its 27 openings (700M nodes, 246s) — refuting the opposite conclusion that time-limited minimax self-play had confidently converged on. A Dueling Double DQN (792K parameters) trained with a curriculum of rule-based opponents reaches 100% against random and bullseye opponents, with training-pipeline pitfalls reported as negative results.
